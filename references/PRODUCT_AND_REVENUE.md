# Chapter-to-product and revenue playbook

## Three jobs, separate KPIs
1. **Attract** with valuable free experiences (chapter previews or approved full text, short films, illustrations). KPI: actual meaningful visits, engaged reads, community responses.
2. **Retain** with truthful onward journeys (next chapter, contents, verified follow channel, optional consent-based newsletter). KPI: return reads, legitimate opt-ins.
3. **Monetize** only where audience need, rights, delivery quality and contribution economics hold. KPI: paid units, net sales, contribution, repeat purchase, royalties where applicable.

## Potential catalog, not a commitment
| Level | Offer concept | Unit/scale | Dependencies | Good chapter fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Digital chapter + film + carousel, behind-the-story notes | Audience | Signed story, functional reader and CTA | Nearly all |
| Entry | Downloadable quote/wallpaper/illustrated mini-edition | Digital | Rights, design, checkout/delivery, willingness-to-pay test | Emotional or visual essays |
| Entry | Art postcards, bookmarks, stickers | Small physical batch or on-demand | Print proof, price, delivery & stock model | Recurring characters/motifs |
| Mid | Illustrated chapbook, art poster or signed print | Print product | High-res master, production QC, rights, packaging | Strongly visual chapters |
| Mid | Themed chapter bundle or audio anthology (own narration) | Digital/print | Multi-chapter approvals, recording clearance | Connected story arcs |
| Premium | Hardcover/collector box, annotated .json edition, limited bundle | Physical + digital | Demand proof, samples, suppliers, unit costs, fulfillment | Book-wide/series |
| Partnership | Publisher edition, translation, licensing, exhibition/talk | Negotiated B2B | Rights ownership, publisher strategy, lawful contracts | Book-wide / suitable essays |

For each story offer **at most three strong revenue hypotheses** instead of creating merch for every chapter. For Cold Rice a gentle illustrated memory postcard/mini print may fit; hard-selling a private family memory may not. Avoid overexposure and respect the author's preference.

## Offer evaluation (1–5; scored as hypothesis)
Score narrative fit 25%, audience desire/evidence 25%, contribution potential 20%, operational feasibility 20%, rights/privacy readiness 10%. Record supporting observations and uncertainties. A product that fails legal/rights/consent may not launch no matter its score.

## Unit economics; do not invent sales or rates
Use VND as operating currency for current pilot unless specified. Define:
- `gross_sales = paid_units * list_price`
- `net_sales = gross_sales - discounts - refunds`
- `variable_costs = manufacturing + packaging + payment/platform fees + outbound shipping subsidy + variable creative/ad spend + other per-order costs`
- `contribution = net_sales - variable_costs`
- `contribution_margin = contribution / net_sales` where net_sales > 0
- `operating_profit_estimate = contribution - allocated_fixed_costs - taxes/other expenses as applicable`
- `break_even_units = ceil(fixed_launch_costs / contribution_per_unit)` when contribution_per_unit > 0

Tag values `observed`, `quote`, `assumption`, or `unknown`. Shipping, fees and tax can change, so verify prior to accepting paid orders. Publisher revenue should model advances/royalties/rights separately from direct-store gross sales.

## Experiment ladder
1. **Interest test:** link click, saves, poll, approved mockups — not paid orders.
2. **Demand validation:** optional waitlist with consent or clear interest form; does not promise inventory.
3. **Prototype:** inspect samples and production quality, verify licensing and defects/reprints.
4. **Commercial test:** a small, legally/operationally ready batch or on-demand offer; clear delivery, returns and refund terms.
5. **Scale or retire:** examine actual gross-to-contribution economics and support burden.

Pilot discipline: current project is a solo founder with AI assistance; AI subscription cited in earlier planning at USD 20/month and pilot cash cap VND 30 million. Treat these as **planning inputs to verify**, not automatic permissions to spend. Prefer low-variable-cost digital or sample-first physical tests, no speculative inventory commitment. Do not imply AI is free just because founder does the work; track hours and opportunity cost separately.

## Funnel specification
For each social placement record date, channel, creative ID, approved landing URL, campaign UTM, observed views/reach when available, actual clicks, reader events (if instrumented), follow/opt-in events, product-page visits, paid units, gross, refunds, variable cost, contribution. Use `unknown` for uninstrumented data; `0` only when there is evidence of zero. Keep paid conversion funnel distinct from editorial reach.

## Commercialization handoff
Every selected offer produces: buyer, problem/desire, emotional/narrative link, proposed SKU/edition, mockup status, rights, price band and evidence class, COGS quote status, fulfillment, sales channel, consent/legal notices, test KPI, experiment stop criteria, founder go/no-go. Payment links and physical products are **never claimed ready** until checkout + delivery + terms are tested.
