# Video creative and production standard

Source: supplied 12-slide `json_video_design_strategy_deck.pptx`, preserved starter docs and the current author-approved book source. See [reconciliation](SOURCE_RECONCILIATION.md) for slide-specific adoption.

## Creative direction

Illustration-led memoir; quiet paper layers, portals, practical dusk light, navy/cream/warm accents, archival serif and small metadata. Preserve the chapter's particular memory and ambiguity. Video invites reading; it does not replace the full story. The proposed 75% illustration / 20% consent-cleared archive / 5% contextualized reconstruction is an unvalidated pilot hypothesis, never a quota or measured result.

Use existing complete E: portals, rectangular art and appropriate cutouts first. Ordinary documentary references require actual provenance/permission. Realistic AI reconstructions need specific creative approval and context; none is generated by this workflow setup. No lip-sync, redrawn faces, fake archival claims or automatic personal-voice synthesis.

## Timing and shots

The deck's 45s HOOK / ENTER / REMEMBER / RECONSIDER / RETURN is a useful shape. Default promotional master range remains 35–60s plus a 12–20s alternate. An explicit full-script request overrides those defaults. Cold Rice's v5 retains the author's complete supplied script and portal-entry introduction: 120 seconds, including 27 story-reading cues, now with living environmental effects. Previous review files and versioned timelines remain available. Keep revelation in the source rather than writing a moral to fill a template beat.

## Opening contract

The [shared opening contract](VIDEO_OPENING.md) applies to **every video**: **whole chapter portal + chapter-title beat → move into its center → reveal the center artwork → begin the story**. Include it in masters, adaptations, recuts and review exports; record explicit author exceptions rather than silently dropping it. A static title card alone does not satisfy this opening. In silent reviews the title is shown as text; do not add synthetic narration. In a later voiced edition, the author's chapter-title recording belongs before the first source paragraph.

V3 holds “Cơm nguội”, begins the camera entry at 2.6 seconds, dissolves from the portal's family-table focus to the existing sharp hero artwork, settles the scene, and starts the story at 10.5 seconds. The first story shots stay inside the revealed scene. Record the focus coordinates, reveal asset/hash and camera scale in the explicit `portal_entry` shot. Check the whole movement and its landing before exporting the full film; preserve the previous review rather than overwriting it.

This is a camera move over existing flat artwork, not genuine separated-plane parallax. The requested opening has its own bounded camera scale (maximum 6×); ordinary story shots retain the quiet 1.00–1.05 limit. The high-resolution hero reveal replaces the magnified center before prolonged enlargement would soften the image.

Each timeline shot records its ID, start/duration, exact VO/citation or labeled adaptation, existing asset/hash, on-screen text and motion. Timing is contiguous and positive. For exact excerpts, the cited line must still contain the words and source SHA must match. Marketing CTA text stays outside memoir.

The flat reading-cut renderer supports contained illustrations, restrained camera pushes, overlapping dissolves and source-checked timed reading cues. Full readings must cover all source paragraphs in order; captions must reproduce each quote and fit their shot. It uses timeline duration and selected dimensions. It does not mix real audio or extract parallax. The separate Cơm nguội v5 composition implements source-aligned layers and procedural vapor, with actual caption-free GIF loops. See its [production guide](../projects/com-nguoi/animation/v5/PRODUCTION.md); other chapters are not automatically converted.

An author may explicitly supply a final video-script variant. Store its exact text and approval scope separately, bind the approval to both video-script and canonical-book hashes, and leave the book selection intact. Cold Rice's v2 records this decision in `projects/com-nguoi/script/final-script-v2-approval.json`. This does not approve public disclosure, final visual quality or release.

Motion target: 2–5% scale change over roughly 5–8s when the shot permits; current preset clamps scale to 1.05 maximum and preserves complete compositions. No aggressive spinning. Keep source-specific rules: the shared main cover fixes Jason while its ring/Gấu move, while Cold Rice's chapter opening retains its own complete artwork. Steam is illustration context and must not label the narrator's cold rice as hot.

## Formats, type and media state

Canonical tokens are in `apps/video/manifests/style.json`: vertical 1080×1920, landscape 1920×1080, square 1080×1080, feed 1080×1440 at 30fps. Use existing Noto Serif with its OFL and verify Vietnamese glyphs. Keep metadata separate from source prose. Check phone/platform control areas in actual upload previews before release; these internal format targets are not claims of current platform eligibility.

Author voice leads final production. Use silence/room tone until a real take is supplied. Select/cite music/SFX licenses independently; existing synthetic ambience is not a real recording of the remembered dinner. Retime final subtitles to actual words/speech, verify pronunciation/diacritics, audio clarity/peaks, crops, image integrity and playback. Silent preview, recorded take, final master and posted asset are different states.

## Runtime handoff

`prepare-video` compiles the selected timeline/cut/format into a private cache and active-preview props. It stages only explicit source art and licensed font; old photos and chapter boards are not a public folder. The v2 reading cut visibly says “Bản dựng xem trước · Không âm thanh”. Render with `npm run render -- --muted` to omit audio entirely. SRT/VTT files from a silent cut describe reading timing, not speech alignment.

The engine retains the starter's Remotion 4.0.332/React 19 stack with pinned versions and a lockfile. Implementation uses [dynamic composition metadata](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/calculate-metadata) and [file-based props for Windows](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/cli/render), checked against official documentation. No upgrade to a new provider/model is required by the deck.

For independently animated scene production, use [json-asset-animation](../../skills/json-asset-animation/SKILL.md). For “alive” or GIF-like art, use its [living-illustration mode](../../skills/json-asset-animation/references/LIVING_ILLUSTRATIONS.md): local subject processes change shape/state, alongside environmental tracks, with a camera-locked loop check. Cơm nguội v5 implements this path through `render:v5`; generic `prepare-video` intentionally refuses its animation timeline. Actual author VO, speech-caption alignment and final mixing remain separate production work. A successful internal render does not clear G2/G3 or create a public URL.
