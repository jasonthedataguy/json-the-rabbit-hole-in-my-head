# Video Creative System

## Product principle
**An illustrated memoir that moves like a memory, sounds like a confession, and opens like a rabbit hole.**

## 45-second episode template
| Seconds | Segment | Image language | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00–05 | Hook | Detail macro or tiny tension, title secondary | First sentence / sound cue |
| 05–14 | Enter portal | Slow camera through layers; no fast spin | Scene anchor |
| 14–28 | Human memory | Center art / archival photo with consent | Specific excerpt |
| 28–39 | Contradiction | Model vs emotion, revelation without moralizing | Reflection |
| 39–45 | Return / CTA | Portal withdraws, restrained `.json` wordmark | Invitation to read |

## Three prototype chapters
- **Cold Rice:** bowl and family table; warmth + small domestic gesture; avoid overstating facts of a particular memory without approved text.
- **Fashion:** Air Jordan 1 and iconic Adidas, then worn-in fabric, personal label; no extra character silhouette.
- **To Saigon:** Ba Son bridge, Bitexco/Landmark 81 silhouettes; backs of couple, pink cap, blonde hair, white dog; nostalgia not tourist advertisement.

## Movement
- Small pan or zoom, 2–5% over 5–8 s; slow opacity transitions; 4–8 visual planes when the approved artwork is extracted.
- Asymmetrical negative space. Motion should lead attention, not compete with narration.
- One quote card maximum unless the chapter specifically calls for data visualization.

## Sound
- Author voice leads. Room-tone, footsteps, faint traffic, keyboard, bowl, rain etc. only when grounded in the scene.
- Use music sparingly, license independently, duck under narration.

## Review
- Authorship and fidelity, people/consent, image integrity, quote accuracy, subtitle spelling/diacritics, sound levels, final playback.
