## COVID and genetics

WHO distinguishes protection against severe disease from prevention of all infection and notes that immunity wanes. Prior exposure or antibodies do not justify describing SARS-CoV-2 as seasonal influenza. [WHO, COVID-19 vaccines, updated 2026-09-15](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/coronavirus-disease-(covid-19)-vaccines).

MedlinePlus describes increased risk of recessive disease where parents are related. This is distinct from social-status preferences and supplies no evidence for the essay's proposed chain from a top-10–20% dating preference to genetic collapse and extinction. [MedlinePlus, Genetics, accessed 2026-10-17](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/002048.htm).

Editorial assessment: the supplied essay has no dataset or quantitative model supporting that chain. Genetic diversity, acquired immune protection and consanguinity are not interchangeable mechanisms. Keep the extinction claim as the narrator's speculative rhetoric; do not promote the board's illustrative graph as measured evidence.
