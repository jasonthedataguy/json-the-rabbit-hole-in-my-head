# Editorial review / Bài 16

## Creative and literary brief

**Thesis:** The narrator starts by accusing others of dating for status, then reveals his own more vulnerable desire: someone who is curious about the texture of his interior life. The extinction warning is his theatrical model, not a scientifically established chain of causation.

**Book role:** A counterpart to *Dating Algorithm* (over-confident theory) and *Three Meeting Theory* (internet romance). Candidate for `03_relationship_modeling` with future echoes in `04_error_analysis`.

**Voice:** raw colloquial Vietnamese with natural Vinglish; preserve jokes, frustration, unfairness, specific objects, tonal whiplash and end-line punch. No invented anecdotes.

### Seven story beats

- **01 · Dating 101 → 102:** Jason declares that dating as status competition will destroy humanity. The stage is grand; his evidence is not.
- **02 · The Status Auction:** Luxury cues, external validation, class signals and the narrator’s zero-sum metaphor.
- **03 · Things You Never Asked:** iPad doodles, living-room light, Thursday football and calf pain; ordinary details carry the argument.
- **04 · Why Do You Think That?:** A remembered Boston art-story with unclear provenance; curiosity feels more intimate than rituals.
- **05 · Wrong Currency:** The PS5/projector/robot vacuum gag and the line about being unbuyable, with a mischievous final aside.
- **06 · Apocalypse Model:** Korean birthrate and genetic collapse as an overconfident, unproven leap. Visual must separate fact from invention.
- **07 · Responsible Dating:** A mock-civic call to date responsibly; the joke lands because the confession came first.

### Book/product visual narrative

- Hero artwork: consultant-style Jason in a suit and clear glasses lecturing beneath a screen that compares STATUS (WRONG CURRENCY) with CURIOSITY (ACTUAL INTIMACY). Two visually smaller scenes reveal a iPad sketch and a grass picnic.
- Keep original imperfect ink/silkscreen texture rather than polished motivational corporate design.
- Main quote: **“Rituals are not intimacy.”** As a narrator’s belief—not a measurement.
- Interactive website concept: **THE CURRENCY EXCHANGE**, with two ways to react to the same ordinary stories. No ranking humans or claiming causal predictions.
- Visual artifact status is **PROOF**: the provided wide infographic is concept art; independent professional illustration masters are not finished.

## Editorial risk ledger

| Original claim / motif | Status | Verification note | Editorial action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Status game is zero-sum | Interpretive / conditional | Competition for relative status can be positional; neither all dating nor relationship welfare is necessarily zero-sum. | Frame as narrator’s model, not universal truth. |
| Materialistic motives for men/women | Broad stereotype | No evidence provided for statements about all women or men. | Preserve stage arrogance while exposing its limits; avoid endorsement. |
| “Curiosity” sixth love language | Pop-culture metaphor | The well-known five love languages framework does not have curiosity as an official sixth category. | Use as felt intimacy, not a validated scientific taxonomy. |
| Boston Impressionism confession | Unverified source | No reliable matching original passage retrieved in this session. | Identify source, seek quotation permission, or recount cautiously without copied wording. |
| Impressionism harder than realism | Speaker opinion | Aesthetic technique comparisons cannot be reduced to an objective hierarchy. | Keep as the story speaker’s claim. |
| Genetic diversity → dating status → extinction | Unsupported causal chain | No basis established for “10–20% top men” causing collapse of human genetic diversity. | Clearly mark as Jason’s speculative exaggeration and critically update it. |
| Covid is now seasonal flu because antibodies | Scientifically misleading | Immunity from infection/vaccination can reduce severe outcomes, but SARS-CoV-2 remains evolving and can cause severe disease. | Correct in editorial fact-check, separate from time-specific voice. |
| South Korea TFR 0.748 | Needs year | 2024: about 0.75; confirmed 2025: 0.80. | Dated footnote rather than silently swapping the narrator’s contemporaneous figure. |
| Vietnam demographic golden period | Supported with qualification | 2024 still in demographic window; aging underway; 2025 TFR 1.93. | Mention within demography notes and keep forecast conditional. |
