# Artwork provenance

Six author-approved conceptual SVG illustrations from the approved Chapter 06 design board. Their editable vector sources remain unchanged. `scripts/render_vector_artwork.py` exports them at 2400 × 1700; `production.json` records each source, export and framing. The card, book, video and platform layouts reuse those exports. The cover SVG keeps typography separately editable. No generated image, movie still or new likeness was added.
