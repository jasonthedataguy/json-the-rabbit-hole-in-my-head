# Play to Win digital-book design adaptation

The author's unchanged `references/design/model_specification - play to win dont play not to lose.png` is the visual source. `scripts/cut_play_to_win_board.py` derives a transparent game-table ring, its aligned centre, a wide table scene and a composition proof. The 486-pixel source circle supports screen display, not a printer-resolution master or independently editable print layers.

The board's **Chapter 06** is the original-edition number. The current digital book keeps this chapter at **03** and restores the author's full English display title, *Play to Win, Don’t Play Not to Lose*. The homepage retains the shared book-cover ring and changes its centre at this stop. The chapter map and reading leaf use this chapter's own ring and scene. The desktop reading preview and chapter leaf show the wide table image. `web/play-to-win-v2.css` carries the navy, teal and warm-paper palette into the original immersive chapter while preserving its game-theory explorer, prose, source figures and historic 06 label.

The selected `source/approved.md` manuscript and original product proofs are unchanged. Print, film, audio and social releases are separate from this screen-art adaptation.
