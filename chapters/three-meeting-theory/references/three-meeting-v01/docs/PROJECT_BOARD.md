## Narrative design

1. **First meeting:** background characters; no invented personal memories.
2. **Second meeting:** spark and bad timing, as romantic theory describes.
3. **Third meeting:** unobserved future, never presented as guaranteed.
4. **Last image:** sunflower-yellow dress; do not append an explanation.

## Rights + ethics

The source is an internet-romantic hypothesis, not scientifically validated. Illustrations are derived from the approved composite moodboard and are for concept/proof use, not distinct high-resolution layered artwork. Any recognizable real-world likeness needs approval.
