# Reproducibility

- The full source for regenerating the EPUB-like website, illustrated proofs, social stills, ambient audio and 24-second video is `build_chapter.py`.
- Requires Python 3, Pillow, reportlab, pypdf, ffmpeg, fonts Noto Serif (not distributed), plus the licensed/approved board crop in `art/00_approved_project_board.jpg`.
- Execute from any directory with `python source/build_chapter.py` after extracting the full archive. This regenerates and overwrites files in the chapter directory.
- The images are adapted from an approved *composite board*, not original independent layered artworks. The board JPEG is included for a reproducible conceptual prototype.
- The 24-second video is an ambient proof, NOT a final narrated TikTok/Reel.
- Run PDF/HTML QA and validate rights before any public use.
