# Three Meeting Theory digital-book design adaptation

The author's board is preserved unchanged at `references/design/relationshio_modelling - three meeting theory.png` (including the source filename's spelling). `scripts/cut_three_meeting_board.py` derives the complete circular collage, homepage centre, rectangular cafe/city vignette and composition proof. The source board has no separate unobstructed river behind the couple. An earlier transparent cutout required an artificial fill that left grey streaks, so the chapter portal now keeps the original bridge, river and both complete figures on one circular sheet.

The homepage retains the common book-cover ring and changes its centre at this stop to the board's complete meeting pair against the sunset bridge. The chapter map and Chapter 17 reading leaf use the dedicated complete circular portal. The whole illustration turns with the reader's drag and settles upright when selected, keeping the figures connected without reconstructing unseen background. The reading leaf and desktop book preview show the full rectangular vignette. The separate immersive page uses the same circular portal and vignette while retaining the approved essay and Third Intersection interaction.

The board's **15** is an original-edition design label. The current digital book retains **Chapter 17**, and original-edition **15** remains historical metadata. The selected `source/approved.md` manuscript and inherited print, film and social proofs are unchanged. The 388-pixel source circle and extracted board collage support this digital adaptation; they are not printer-resolution or separately editable print layers.
