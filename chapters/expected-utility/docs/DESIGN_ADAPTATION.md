# Expected Utility digital-book design adaptation

The current digital art comes from the author's revised `references/design/model_specification - expected utility.png`. `scripts/cut_expected_utility_v3_board.py` cuts a transparent ring of branching paths and choices while keeping Jason's head and body together, an aligned stationary gaze, a face-only centre for the shared homepage ring, a wide gaze panorama and the full extracted-object strip. The standalone page uses the same portal and gaze panorama as the book. These are screen assets from a flattened board, not separable print masters.

The previous `references/design/Expected Utility_ Portal Layers Board.png` and its v2 crops remain preserved as earlier design work.

The board's **Chapter 04** is the original edition number. The current digital book retains Expected Utility as **Chapter 02**. The homepage keeps the shared book-cover ring and changes only its centre at this stop. The chapter map and reading leaf use Expected Utility's own ring and centre; the desktop preview and standalone reader show the wide gaze panorama. The full object strip remains a reusable digital crop. Its original-edition number, source essay, print proof, film and product links remain distinct from the current edition. `source/approved.md` is unchanged.
