# Narrative map

| Paragraphs | Layer | Visual treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Dreamlike framing | Paris dusk / symbolic rooftop motif. |
| 3–7 | Observation and partial understanding | Suitcases, Line 5, two children, skateboard, phone. |
| 8–11 | Imagined life | A yellow-lit kitchen and family dinner; clearly labelled imagined. |
| 12–13 | Limits of knowledge | The narrator explicitly does not know whether those details are real. |
| 14–18 | Afterimage | Amber, low contrast, grain, later edited photographs. |
| 19 | Original ending | Keep the exact closing sentence; no invented coda. |

The center is the gap between what is seen and what the mind fills in. The Borrowed Reel makes that distinction visible through three modes and a color-grade slider. The manuscript stays unchanged. The symbolic rooftop animal and hair colors are illustration choices; they are not added to the narrative as facts. The phone artwork is a concept and does not match the described old monochrome keypad handset exactly.
