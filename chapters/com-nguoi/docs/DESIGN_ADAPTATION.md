# Cold Rice digital-book design adaptation

The author's unchanged `references/design/prologue - cold rice.png` is the visual reference. `scripts/cut_cold_rice_board.py` cuts its floral lattice ring, aligned family-dinner centre and wide reading image. The source circle is about 392 pixels across, so these are screen assets, not printer-resolution layers. `docs/qa/cold-rice-board-portal-composition.png` is an alignment proof.

The chapter map, prologue leaf and immersive chapter use the Cold Rice portal. The homepage retains the shared book-cover ring and changes its centre to this board's dinner scene. The desktop book preview and the prologue leaf use the wide family-room image. The immersive page's navy, cream, teal and amber palette follows the board while retaining its original interactive sections, selected prose and original product links.

The current digital book numbers Cold Rice **Prologue 00**; the original essay was **07**. The board's mock chapter page displays **03** as an illustrative layout number, not the book's editorial or original-edition number. `source/approved.md`, historical artwork, inherited print proof, audio and film are preserved. This change publishes a digital adaptation only.
