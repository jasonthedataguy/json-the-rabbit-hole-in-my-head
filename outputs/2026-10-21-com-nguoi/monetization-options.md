## H1 — digital keepsake, first option to assess

For an existing reader who wants an offline illustrated object. Possible SKU `COM-NGUOI-MINI-V1`, concept only: approved full prose, existing hero/inline art and optional printable art page. Reuse the A5 proof as an input; its old edition references and layout need review before becoming a paid export. Do not invent extra family memories, exclusivity or personal dedications.

After disclosure approval, privately show a sample to five willing readers and ask what format they would use and whether/what they would pay. Five is a proposed research sample, not an audience count. Proposed discovery rule: continue if three express concrete interest and two give usable willingness-to-pay answers; this is not proof of market demand. Stop if responses value only the free story or commercialization feels wrong to the author. Price VND, payment fees, delivery/support and buyer terms: unknown. Tested download/checkout is not configured.

## Selection, economics and G4

Select at most one first experiment. H1 is first on practical production grounds, not proven demand. Every numeric price/cost later needs an `observed`, `quote` or `assumption` label. For digital, include transaction/platform, variable delivery/support and creative costs; physical adds manufacture, packaging and shipping subsidy. Contribution = net sales minus variable costs. Break-even units = fixed launch cost / positive contribution per unit, rounded up. Contribution is not operating profit; fixed costs and applicable taxes/other expenses remain separate. No numerical profit forecast is warranted now.

G4 requires exact SKU/edition, current price/cost evidence, buyer terms, rights, physical sample where applicable, tested payment/delivery and explicit founder approval. Old cash-cap/subscription planning inputs are not spending authority. Real units/revenue/refunds/costs stay unknown until observed.
