EDITION NOTES

About this edition

The book moves from the prologue, Cold Rice, through five parts and thirty-two chapters, following the narrator’s changing view of the world.

The original Vinglish manuscripts are preserved. English titles guide navigation; unpublished chapters are marked as outlines. Book numbers and original publication numbers belong to different editions.

The printed book

The blue cloth cover, circular windows and layered paper are concepts for a physical edition in development. These images show design studies, not a manufactured book.
