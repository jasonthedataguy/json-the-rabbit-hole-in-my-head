## The transmedia product

1. **Physical memoir:** truthful full text, beautiful illustrated chapter-openers, tactile navy cover. **Single cover aperture**, five reinforced act-gate treatments or printed depth, and chapter illustrations. Do not die-cut holes through every prose page.
2. **Digital reader:** the unchanged manuscript; shared portal ring and stationary scene; `Read → Explore → Revisit`; optional artifacts, no reading-critical interaction. Deep links must take visitors into the exact chapter mentioned in each video. Reduced motion and keyboards are essential.
3. **Discovery films:** every episode opens behind the small Jason silhouette facing the portal, then enters the actual chapter world. Short story first; chapter CTA last. Film is an acquisition door, not a substitute manuscript.
4. **Collectibles:** limited chapter art, postcards, bookmarks, five-act prints, and only demand-validated collector binding/box. No unauthorized band/celebrity/shoe logos.
5. **Business:** free taste via reader → confirmed opt-in → refundable preorder → quoted and rights-cleared print run. Avoid claiming paid demand before deposits.

## Physical spec decisions needing approval

`shared/design.json` uses A5 as the current editorial proof (148×210mm). The older atlas proposes 170×240mm, and another concept proposed 6×9in. These are incompatible trim specs; **A5 is the current proof baseline, final manufacturing trim TBD**. Test a cover hole, one act gate and a Fashion spread with a printer before locking size, spine, stock or die diameter. Have a supplier sign off on the aperture location, strength, waste rate and binding creep.
