# Video storytelling system — one portal, 36 distinctive memories

## Core film signature

Every short starts with **the same back-facing Jason silhouette** at the navy circular portal. The silhouette never has to be face-recognizable. For 1–2 seconds the viewer sees stillness. The inner aperture changes to the chapter's unique memory. The camera moves through the ring. The voice tells a **specific remembered scene**, never a book advertisement or generic productivity advice.

## Universal 60-second edit

| Timestamp | Beat | Visual/sound | Editing contract |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00–02 | Silhouette | Jason at threshold, navy paper | same mark every chapter |
| 02–05 | Story cue | cold rice / shoes / Ba Son bridge / eyes in code | specific scene; no title-card delay |
| 05–13 | Sensory detail | one tactile event and first-person narration | truth and source trace |
| 13–36 | Complication | suspense, desire, joke or error | no artificial drama |
| 36–51 | Shift | a contradiction or feeling | don't force moral lesson |
| 51–58 | Resonance | image echoes in ring; silence permitted | leave room for reader |
| 58–60 | CTA | chapter name and full-memory link | simple, unobtrusive |

## Platform packages

- 9:16 vertical master (1080×1920), 30–75 seconds, platform-safe subtitles for TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts.
- YouTube 4–8-minute narrated essays when the story justifies it, not automatically for every chapter.
- Six-card Instagram sequence using chapter art, an exact attributed excerpt and direct link.
- Original poster and transcript on the chapter destination.
- Per-platform campaign tags: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign=<slug>`, `utm_content=<variant>`.

## First four films

1. **Cold Rice (Prologue 00):** family meal, one bowl of leftover rice, a child's attempt to spare his father. Slow warm rhythm. Ending is a remembered action, not a theory.
2. **Fashion (04):** shoe-boxes and iconography of passing trends → father’s inherited shirt. Rhythmic early cuts → still human close. Original sneaker silhouettes, no borrowed celebrity footage.
3. **To My Saigon (14):** arriving with luggage, seeing Ba Son bridge, uncertain job, social warmth and loss. Let dusk and city textures carry change.
4. **Expected Utility (02):** debugging at night, the pair of eyes reflected in the window, choice paths that resist clean resolution. Keep intellectual model as narratorial voice, not scientific prescription.

## Asset pipeline per episode

`approved.md` + consent checklist → 45–60s author-reviewed script → shot list/animatic → chapter-specific ring/scene layers → author reading or clearly consented narration → original/cleared sound → timed human-reviewed SRT/VTT → silent mobile review → 1080×1920 master + captioned variant → landing URL/UTM → analytics and postmortem.

### Non-negotiables

Do not infer a real colleague's motive, recreate private chat logs as fact, fabricate data, or claim a story is directly quoted when the video hook was editorially invented. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not autoplay audio on the site. No copyrighted song samples, recognizable film stills or uncleared brand logos. One episode is not released just because a concept board exists.

## Growth pilot (90 days)

3 canonical reader destinations, 6–9 story variants, minimum 20 reader interviews, supplier proof, opt-in/deposit funnel and actual contribution tracking. Measure sessions arriving to the *correct chapter*, engaged reads, confirmed opt-ins, paid orders and refunds. Optimize for repeat reading and eventual purchasers, not only views.
