# Reading order and front matter

The publisher narrative review (`json_publisher_narrative_review.pdf.pdf`, supplied by the author) controls the reading structure of this edition:

1. Title, epigraph, and editorial introduction.
2. Prologue — Cơm nguội (editorial ID 00).
3. I — Những mô hình đầu tiên / model_specification (01–05).
4. II — Những thứ không thể đo / data_collection (06–15).
5. III — Mô hình của tình yêu / relationship_modeling (16–21).
6. IV — Một mô hình tự chứng minh mình đúng / model_failure (22–26).
7. V — Những thứ bị bỏ sót / under_revision (27–31).
8. Lời xin lỗi (31) also serves as the epilogue.

The review contains no separate author-written preface. `dist/book.js` uses the review's epigraph and three non-spoiler excerpts from section I as editorial introductory material. The page identifies this as a lead-in from the work's outline. It does not present the publisher's complete synopsis, later plot revelations, market positioning, or editing recommendations as the book's opening.

Published manuscript bytes and all source provenance remain unchanged. Only rendering omits each manuscript's duplicated first heading; the reader heading uses the new book title and records a different published title when applicable. Missing manuscripts show editorial premises from section II as **Đề cương**, never invented full prose.

The website homepage is the illustrated chapter map. The introduction remains the first page in the book’s reading sequence and is accessible through `#introduction`. Explicit chapter links use `#chapter/00` through `#chapter/31`, the printed-style contents use `#contents`, and the illustrated chapter wheel uses `#map` and is also shown at the root URL. Original published editions remain separately accessible under `#archive`. Physical-book design material lives under `#edition`.
